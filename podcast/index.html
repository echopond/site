A House-Shaped House

The Hard Work of Building Together

Chris R. Glass

8 chapters · 2 hr 0 min

RSS feed

A resolved house compresses its history. It turns a choice into an appearance, a negotiation into a detail, and a sequence of provisional judgments into something that can look inevitable. This audiobook stays with one house and looks beneath the finished image: a first walk of the land conducted by phone, a brief written as life, a road that had to be paid for before it could be drawn, a form that was simple to draw and difficult to build, and the year after completion, when the house changed tense.
